## Sample Questions

### Question 1 (Multiple Choice)
What would the nurse document after assessing a neonate born at 38 weeks' gestation who weighs 8 lb 13.1 oz (4,017 g)?

**Answer:** B) Large for gestational age, term.
- Rationale: Birth weight >4,000g = LGA; 38 weeks = term

### Question 4 (SATA)
A pregnant client is receiving magnesium sulfate to prevent eclampsia via a continuous intravenous infusion. Hourly assessments are recorded. Which assessment findings lead the nurse to stop the infusion immediately? Select all that apply.

**Answer:** B, D, F
- Absent deep tendon reflexes (DTR)
- Urine output of 10 mL/hr
- Respiratory rate of 10 breaths per minute
- Rationale: These indicate magnesium toxicity

### Question 11 (Numeric)
The provider orders magnesium sulfate to treat a primigravida client's severe pre-eclampsia. The medication is available as 60 grams of magnesium sulfate in 1000 mL of lactated ringer's at a rate of 4 grams/hr. What is the starting rate of the infusion?

**Answer:** 67 mL/hr
- Calculation: (4 g/hr × 1000 mL) ÷ 60 g = 66.67 mL/hr → 67 mL/hr

### Question 26 (Numeric)
The provider orders magnesium sulfate to treat a primigravida client's severe pre-eclampsia. The medication is available as 60 grams of magnesium sulfate in 1000 mL of Lactated Ringer's at a rate of 4 grams/hr. The medication is titrated every hour by 1 gram/hr. The infusion began at 0800, what is the rate of infusion at 1300.

**Answer:** 150 mL/hr
- Calculation: 
  - Starting rate: 4 g/hr = 67 mL/hr
  - Time elapsed: 0800 to 1300 = 5 hours
  - Titrations: 5 × 1 g/hr = 5 g/hr increase
  - New rate: 4 + 5 = 9 g/hr
  - Volume: (9 g/hr × 1000 mL) ÷ 60 g = 150 mL/hr

### Question 33 (Multiple Choice)
Assessment of a primigravida at 32 weeks' gestation shows a blood pressure of 160/110 mm Hg, 4+ proteinuria and edema of the face and extremities. Which complication are these findings consistent with?

**Answer:** A) Preeclampsia with severe features.
- Rationale: BP ≥160/110 + 4+ proteinuria = severe preeclampsia

### Question 37 (Multiple Choice)
The nurse assesses a client in labor due to a noted prolonged fetal heart rate deceleration (HR 118 bpm) on the monitor. Upon entering the room, the client reports "a feeling of something weird between her legs," and the nurse notes an umbilical cord prolapse upon assessment. What is the priority nursing intervention when umbilical cord prolapse is noted?

**Answer:** D) Positioning the client to relieve pressure on the prolapsed cord, such as the Trendelenburg or knee-chest position.
- Rationale: Immediate positioning to relieve cord compression is priority
