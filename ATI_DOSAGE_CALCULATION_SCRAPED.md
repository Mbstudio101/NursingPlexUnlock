## 🎯 Sample Questions

### Numeric Calculation Examples:

**Question 1:** Morphine 0.2 mg/kg IM, client weighs 99 lb, available 10 mg/mL
- Convert 99 lb to kg: 99 ÷ 2.2 = 45 kg
- Calculate dose: 0.2 mg/kg × 45 kg = 9 mg
- Calculate volume: 9 mg ÷ 10 mg/mL = **0.9 mL**

**Question 7:** Metronidazole 7.5 mg/kg PO, client weighs 146.7 lb, available 500 mg tablets
- Convert 146.7 lb to kg: 146.7 ÷ 2.2 = 66.7 kg
- Calculate dose: 7.5 mg/kg × 66.7 kg = 500 mg
- Calculate tablets: 500 mg ÷ 500 mg = **1 tablet**

**Question 22:** Methylprednisolone 1.7 mg/kg/day IM divided q12h, child weighs 64 lb, available 20 mg/mL
- Convert 64 lb to kg: 64 ÷ 2.2 = 29.1 kg
- Calculate daily dose: 1.7 mg/kg × 29.1 kg = 49.5 mg/day
- Calculate per dose: 49.5 mg ÷ 2 = 24.75 mg
- Calculate volume: 24.75 mg ÷ 20 mg/mL = **1.2 mL per dose**

## 🎓 Key Learning Points

This exam covers critical nursing calculation skills:

### Weight-Based Dosing:
- Convert lb to kg (divide by 2.2)
- Calculate dose: mg/kg × weight in kg
- Calculate volume: dose ÷ concentration

### Unit Conversions:
- 1 kg = 2.2 lb
- 1 g = 1,000 mg
- 1 mg = 1,000 mcg
- 1 L = 1,000 mL
- 1 tsp = 5 mL

### Rounding Rules:
- Nearest tenth: one decimal place
- Nearest hundredth: two decimal places
- Nearest whole number: no decimal places
- Use leading zero (0.5, not .5)
- No trailing zeros (5, not 5.0)

### Prescription Types:
- **PRN:** As needed
- **Standard:** Routine scheduled medication
- **Single:** One-time dose
- **Now:** Immediate, one-time order
